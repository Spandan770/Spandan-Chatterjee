Things you can try right now

Slow breathing (about 2 minutes)

Watch the circle. Breathe in while it grows, hold for a moment, breathe out slowly while it shrinks. Repeat about 10 times.

More quick help

Mind racing? Try 5-4-3-2-1

Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell and 1 you can taste. It pulls your attention back to the room.

Too much to study
  • Write down everything, then pick the one thing due first.
  • Study 25 minutes, rest 5 minutes, repeat.
  • Ask your professor for help early, before a deadline.
Can't sleep
  • Wake up at the same time every day.
  • Keep your phone away for the last 30 minutes before bed.
  • Go for a short walk. It helps more than studying tired.
How do I start talking to someone?

You can simply say: "I've been struggling lately and could use some help." That's enough to start.
